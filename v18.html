About — PAI Launcher v2.5

⬢ What it is — a circular desktop widget (Electron) that acts as the launch pad for Dane's Personal AI Infrastructure. The frameless round window floats over the desktop; everything outside the circle is click-through.
◉ What it does — 14 project circles ride a rotating ratchet ring (Email Agent, Todoist, Health, Spinners, Council, and more). Clicking one opens that project's web app at its localhost port in its own window. The center hub launches PAI, Claude, and Hermes.
✥ How to use it — drag the ring to rotate (it clicks into place); click a circle to open it; drag the dark void to move the window; ＋/－ zoom the circle between 400 and 1400 px; − minimizes; × or the small red X on the hub closes; ? shows this screen. Escape or a click dismisses overlays.
⚙ How it works — plain HTML, CSS, and canvas JavaScript, no framework. Snap physics with a Web Audio click drive the ring. An Electron IPC bridge (preload.js → main.js) handles window move, smooth resize, minimize, and close. Launch it from the PAI menu (P2) or the orbital command.
